04 / Equipment questions

What to clarify before ordering.

Does automatic lifting mean a fully automatic line?

No. Vertical lifting, horizontal transfer, loading and unloading may have different automation levels. List each function separately.

Can throughput be stated from tank count?

No. Use the validated stage times, basket pitch, transfer strategy, draining, loading and changeover assumptions.

Which numerical specifications are published here?

The available catalogue does not publish an order-specific automatic-line rating. Use a project technical schedule; no capacity or load rating is inferred from the image.

What is needed before a layout?

Part drawings, loaded carrier geometry and weight, required output, route, floor space, utilities and acceptance method.
