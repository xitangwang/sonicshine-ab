01 / Suitability

Confirm the part and process boundary.

  • Parts needing a defined rinse or dry condition after cleaning.
  • Loads that can be supported consistently between process stages.
  • Processes where contamination carryover must be reviewed.
  • Water, drain, ventilation and space requirements can be agreed for each stage.
02 / Process & configuration

Every function has a purpose.

01

Choose the stage functions

F2, F2S and F2F represent different two-stage combinations. Specify cleaning, rinse, spray and dry functions in the quotation.

02

Keep rinse separate

Rinse quality, flow and maintenance depend on carryover and the next manufacturing step.

03

Review drying carefully

Where drying is offered, evaluate temperature limits, trapped water and the loaded-basket arrangement.

04

Define manual or assisted transfer

Basket lift, crane assistance and automatic transfer are separate functions that must be stated in the scope.

04 / Equipment questions

What to clarify before ordering.

Does F4 always mean exactly four cleaning tanks?

No. Treat it as the catalogue’s multi-tank series designation; confirm both the number and function of process positions.

Are all F3 positions ultrasonic?

No. The catalogue includes a hot-air-drying position. The F3G arrangement includes spraying. Confirm the selected process.

Are the listed powers totals for the whole line?

The source table does not establish that interpretation. Confirm power per stage, simultaneous load and connected electrical demand.

Can a rinse simply be skipped?

Assess residual chemistry, particle carryover, material compatibility and the next operation before removing a stage.
