Understand how contact links and the enquiry form work.

Contact information use

Only the details needed for the discussion.

The static website does not automatically transmit the enquiry form. Email and WhatsApp buttons prepare a message addressed to the listed business contact; you review and send it in your chosen application.

If a separately configured receiving service is enabled, the form identifies that submission mode and reports success only after that service accepts it. Your name, contact details and project text are then used to respond to the enquiry.

No newsletter opt-in is required. Do not send confidential drawings until sharing arrangements are agreed; photos and PDFs can be attached directly to your email.

Third-party email, WhatsApp and hosting services handle the information under their own policies. Contact postmaster@gzsonicshine.com about information you have sent.
