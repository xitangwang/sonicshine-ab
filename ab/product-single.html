01 / Suitability

Confirm the part and process boundary.

  • Machining oil and process residue on compatible metal parts.
  • A defined batch with loading, rinsing and drying responsibilities already identified.
  • Parts can be supported with solution access, without resting on the tank floor.
  • Sensitive alloys, coatings, adhesives and trapped assemblies require a compatibility review.
02 / Process & configuration

Every function has a purpose.

01

Stainless steel tank

The supplied F-series catalogue describes SUS304 construction. Confirm the contact materials and chemical compatibility for the order.

02

Heating and timed control

Catalogue models list heating, time settings and adjustable ultrasonic power. The operating recipe remains part-specific.

03

Filtration choice

The F+ reference arrangement adds filtration. Define the contaminant, filter access and service interval in the scope.

04

Load handling

Define a basket and fixture first. Discuss lift assistance only after total weight, travel and access are known.

04 / Equipment questions

What to clarify before ordering.

Which tank dimension determines whether my parts fit?

Use the effective loaded basket envelope, including fixtures and clearance. Catalogue tank dimensions are not usable basket dimensions.

Does 88 L mean an 88 L operating fill?

Not necessarily. Treat catalogue capacity as a published reference and confirm the working level, displacement and overflow allowance.

Is 28/40 kHz simultaneous dual-frequency operation?

The catalogue uses that notation without defining the mode. Confirm the offered frequency and operating mode in the written specification.

Is filtration standard on every F unit?

No such blanket assumption should be made. The catalogue distinguishes F, FH and F+; confirm the exact bill of configuration.
