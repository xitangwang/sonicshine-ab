Cleaning glossary

Clear terms.
Fewer assumptions.

A short reference for equipment and process discussions.

Terminology

Use the same definitions.

English term中文Meaning
Cavitation空化Formation and collapse of small cavities in a liquid under acoustic pressure changes; part of the ultrasonic cleaning mechanism.
Working envelope有效工作空间Space actually available to the loaded basket and fixtures under the intended working conditions.
Nominal capacity公称容量A published capacity convention; confirm whether it represents geometric or working liquid volume.
Carryover工位间携带Liquid and contamination moved with parts or baskets into the next stage.
Rinsing漂洗A process stage intended to reduce remaining cleaning liquid or residue according to the required output.
Lift assistance升降辅助A vertical basket-handling function; it does not by itself imply automatic transfer or unloading.
Frequency频率An acoustic operating variable, usually expressed in kHz; confirm the available mode for the specific equipment.
Ultrasonic power超声功率A value that requires a definition, such as stated generator, input, peak or average power; do not mix conventions.
Acceptance method验收方法The agreed way to check a specified result, with representative parts and defined observation or measurement conditions.
