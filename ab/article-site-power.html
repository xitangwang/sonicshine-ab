Confirming electrical supply before ordering a cleaning system

Voltage alone is not a complete electrical specification. Bring the site electrician into the review early.

Quick answer

Voltage alone is not a complete electrical specification. Bring the site electrician into the review early.

Record the actual site supply

Ask the site’s qualified electrical contact to identify the available voltage, phase arrangement and frequency at the intended connection. Record the installation location and any local approval requirements. A country name or a photograph of an outlet is not enough to define the supply for industrial equipment. Do not choose protective devices from a generic web guide.

Read the machine demand correctly

Ultrasonic power, heater power, dryer power and connected electrical demand are different values. Determine which functions operate simultaneously and whether the supplier’s figure is per stage or for the whole machine. Ask for the current electrical schedule and relevant drawing. Never add catalogue numbers together and present the result as an approved installation rating.

Review the full utility list

Depending on the chosen process, the equipment may also need water, drainage, ventilation or other services. Confirm those interfaces and responsibility for installation work. Review access for isolation and maintenance as part of the layout. A suitable floor footprint does not by itself show that the equipment can be connected or serviced.

Keep destination requirements project-specific

Electrical and conformity requirements depend on the equipment scope, installation and destination. Obtain advice from the responsible local professionals and request the supplier’s applicable documents. This article does not state a universal certification requirement for any country and does not treat a logo on a brochure as proof of approval.

Close the interface before dispatch

Record the agreed supply and utilities in the approved order specification. Check that the delivered model and its drawings match that record. Any difference should be resolved before installation or energizing. The practical outcome is a shared, written connection plan rather than an assumption based on the equipment family name.
