02 / Process assessment

A process to evaluate, not a fixed recipe.

  1. 01

    Review the approved scope

    Identify drawings, material restrictions and controlled process requirements.

  2. 02

    Record trial conditions

    Use traceable samples, load details and permitted operating settings.

  3. 03

    Protect the surface

    Review fixtures, contact points, rinsing and drying with the responsible engineer.

  4. 04

    Document acceptance

    Retain the agreed inspection record and any unresolved limitations.

The sequence is assessment guidance. Chemistry, time, temperature and any rinse or drying stage must be validated for the actual material and residue.

04 / Application questions

The details worth resolving early.

Does an application page mean aerospace approval?

No. Customer approval, process qualification and document scope must be established for the particular project.

Can a protective coating remain unchanged?

Assess the actual coating system, damage limits and representative samples before setting conditions.

Which documents are useful first?

Material identity, drawing revision, approved cleaning restrictions and acceptance procedure.

How should a trial be recorded?

Record the sample identifier, residue, recipe, load orientation, inspection method and deviations.
